    More from the workbench

      Barbara and her wool

      Ten years of felting

      Barbara grew up in Germany and now lives in the Derbyshire countryside. She has loved making things for as long as she can remember, and has been needle and wet felting for about ten years.

      British wool, dyed at home

      Most of her fleece is locally sourced British wool that she dyes herself, with merino and silk for the finer pieces. She also throws raku ceramics for buttons and little houses, and crochets with handspun wool.

      Visit the Etsy shop

      Your dog, in wool

      Barbara makes felted miniatures of real dogs, built on a wire frame so they can be posed. Most are about 14 cm long, depending on the breed.

      1. Send a few photos

        Side, front and a favourite expression. Tell her about markings the photos miss.

      2. Watch it take shape

        She sends progress photos while she works, so you can check the face and colours.

      3. Posed and posted

        The finished dog is posed the way you like and sent to you, wrapped with care.

      Dog portraits from £48. A West Highland terrier is £30.
